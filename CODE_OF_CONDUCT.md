# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Threadlight contributors commit to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community. We value clear technical disagreement, good-faith collaboration, and empathy for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Giving specific, constructive feedback.
- Focusing discussion on ideas and observable behavior.
- Acknowledging mistakes and helping repair their impact.
- Respecting privacy, boundaries, and project decisions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers who are involved in a report must recuse themselves from handling it.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.

This document is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
